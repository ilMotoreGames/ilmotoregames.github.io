Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the following mobile games created by ilMotoreGames (hereby referred to as "Service Provider", "we" or "us"), each hereby referred to as an "Application":

  • Almost Ever After (Android, package com.ilmotoregames.almosteverafter)
  • Bullets & Bites (Android, package com.ilmotoregames.bulletsAndBites)

The Applications are ad-supported and are provided "AS IS". The Service Provider is an independent developer based in Chile. Sections that apply to a single game are marked as such; everything else applies to all of them.

Almost Ever After

What we do not collect

Almost Ever After does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. You never type free text that is sent to other players: in-game messages between paired players are chosen from a fixed list, and only a message code travels over the network.

Information the Application processes
  • Anonymous account identifier. When you first open the game, an anonymous account is created automatically through Firebase Authentication. It is a random identifier, not linked to your name or Google account, and is used to store your progress in the cloud.
  • Game progress. Your saved game (character, appearance, home, furniture, in-game money and loans, relationship progress and similar game data) is stored in Cloud Firestore under that identifier so you do not lose it if you change devices or reinstall.
  • Multiplayer data. If you use the couple mode, the game shares with the player you are paired with (and, while searching for a partner, with other players looking for one) your in-game character's name, appearance and game stats, the shared household data, and the codes of the predefined messages and reactions you send. Temporary invitation codes are stored until they are used or expire.
  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns your device a token so the game can notify you (for example, when your partner sends you a message). You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.
  • Usage and diagnostic data. Through Google Analytics for Firebase we receive information about how the game is used (for example, game events, session length, device model, operating system version, app version and approximate country derived from the IP address). We use it to fix problems and balance the game.
  • Advertising data. Rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information to show ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads are personalized only where you have given consent where the law requires it (see "Your choices").
  • Purchases. If in-app purchases are offered, payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation of what was purchased.
Why we use it
  • To provide the game, save your progress and make multiplayer work (necessary to provide the service you request).
  • To understand usage, fix bugs and improve the game (our legitimate interest).
  • To show ads that keep the game free; personalized ads only with your consent where required.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it to contact you for marketing.

Your choices
  • Ad consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where it is required, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can review or withdraw it at any time from the Privacy button in the game's Settings.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Notifications. You can disable them in your device settings.

Bullets & Bites

Information Collection and Use

Bullets & Bites collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

For a better experience, the Service Provider may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to Email. That information will be retained and used as described in this privacy policy, and may be used to contact you with important information and required notices.

Bullets & Bites shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID as described in the Almost Ever After section above.

Applies to all Applications

Google's servers may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Google provides safeguards for these transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Disclosure

We may disclose information only:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • to the service providers listed above, who work on our behalf.
Data Retention and Deletion

Cloud saves and multiplayer data are kept while you play and for a reasonable time afterwards so you can recover your progress. Analytics data is kept according to the retention period configured in Google Analytics. Data held by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies.

You can ask us to delete your data at any time by writing to ilmotoregames@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and the name of the game. For Almost Ever After, please include your Player ID, shown in the game's Settings (use the "Copy" button next to it); since the game does not ask for your name or email, it is the only way we can find your saved game. We will confirm the deletion within 30 days. Uninstalling the game stops further collection but does not by itself delete your cloud save.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before. This includes the rights granted by the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and by Chilean law (Law 19.628 and, from its entry into force, Law 21.719). To exercise them, contact us at the address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

The Applications are not directed to children under the age of 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at ilmotoregames@gmail.com.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on Google's infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.
